Applied AI

From experimentation to enterprise impact

Redesign the path from business need to scalable value.

The mindset shift: It’s not about how AI can make the current path faster. It’s about how the path itself should transform now that AI can participate throughout.

Business experts can directly create potential solutions

  • People closest to a business problem can now use AI to shape a working solution directly.
  • The starting point shifts from requiring multiple handoffs to translate the need to business experts exploring, testing, and refining their solution ideas in context.

Beware the enterprise chasm

  • A useful prototype is not yet a production-grade enterprise capability.
  • Scaling prototypes introduces challenges such as permissions, governance, security, architecture, deployment standards, and cost controls.

Enterprise knowledge closes the gap

  • General AI knows how software is built. Enterprise-aware AI understands company-specific patterns, policies, controls, and internal requirements.
  • Bringing that knowledge directly to the business user allows governance and engineering standards to shape the work earlier and more effectively.

Three moves to create enterprise value

  1. 01

    Redesign the workflow

    Replace sequential handoffs with a model in which business experts can shape solutions directly while enterprise knowledge and controls guide delivery.

  2. 02

    Elevate the human role

    Business users focus on the need and the outcome. Engineers focus on reusable platforms, governance frameworks, automation, enterprise patterns, and complex engineering.

  3. 03

    Scale value responsibly

    Create a repeatable path from isolated experiments to secure, governed, monitored, cost-aware capabilities that can be shared across the enterprise.
